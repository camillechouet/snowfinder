4. Privacy policy & GDPR

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679) and the French Data Protection Act (Loi Informatique et Libertés), we inform you about the collection and processing of your personal data.

Data collected

SnowFinder only collects anonymous browsing data via Google Analytics:

  • Pages visited and visit duration
  • Country and city of origin (approximate)
  • Device type and browser used
  • Traffic source (Google search, direct link, etc.)

⚠️ No identifying personal data (name, first name, email address, full IP address) is collected without your explicit consent. IP addresses are anonymised.

Data submitted via the contact form

If you use our contact form, the details you enter (email, message) are used only to reply to your request and are not kept for more than 12 months.

Legal basis for processing

  • Analytics: Legitimate interest (improving the service)
