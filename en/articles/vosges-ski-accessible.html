Vosges: skiing within 2h of Paris and Strasbourg

✍️ SnowFinder Editorial📅 January 2025⏱ 3 min read

No need to cross France to ski. The Vosges offer simple, cosy and very affordable skiing, ideal for a spontaneous weekend away.

Gérardmer, the jewel of the Vosges

On the shore of Lake Gérardmer, the largest resort in the Vosges offers 40 km of varied runs with a snowpark. All in a unique lakeside setting in France.

La Bresse-Hohneck

Facing the highest point of the Vosges, complementary alpine and Nordic ski areas. The most complete resort in the massif for families.

Tip

The Vosges are prone to warm spells. Check the snow conditions before you leave and aim for January-February for the best conditions.
