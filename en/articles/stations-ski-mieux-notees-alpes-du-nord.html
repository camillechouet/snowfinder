The 10 best-rated ski resorts in the French Northern Alps

✍️ SnowFinder Editorial Team📅 26 October 2026⏱ 8 min

Which resorts are the best rated in the French Northern Alps? Here is the ranking based on the SnowFinder score, which combines the ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere and facilities. We reviewed the 85 resorts in our database in the French Northern Alps that qualify for this ranking.

Key takeaway: Val Thorens is the top-rated (rating 4.9/5), ahead of Chamonix (rating 4.9/5) · the 10th in the ranking, Alpe d'Huez, scores 4.5/5.

The ranking: 10 resorts

1Val Thorens

rating 4.9/5
1235308

SnowFinder score of 4.9/5, for 150 km of runs between 2,300 and 3,230 m. Adult day lift pass: €74.

Val Thorens is Europe's highest resort at 2,300 m, at the top of the 3 Vallées (600 km). This altitude guarantees good-quality snow from November to May. A modern, lively and very busy resort, it is popular with young skiers thanks to its many bars and clubs at the foot of the slopes. Its own ski area has 85 runs over 150 km.

2Chamonix

rating 4.9/5
16434416

SnowFinder score of 4.9/5, for 110 km of runs between 1,035 and 3,842 m. Adult day lift pass: €77.

Chamonix-Mont-Blanc is the world capital of mountaineering, nestled at 1,035 m at the foot of Mont Blanc (4,808 m). Its 170 km ski area is spread across Les Grands Montets, Le Brévent and La Flégère. The Vallée Blanche (24 km of off-piste) is the most legendary glacier descent in the world. A demanding destination, wilder than the big purpose-built resorts.

3Val d'Isère

rating 4.8/5
15332211

SnowFinder score of 4.8/5, for 150 km of runs between 1,850 and 3,456 m. Adult day lift pass: €70.

Val d'Isère is one of the most legendary resorts in the Alps, perched at 1,850 m in the Haute-Tarentaise. Together with Tignes, it forms the Tignes-Val d'Isère ski area (300 km), which hosted the 1992 Olympic Games. The Pisaillas glacier rises to 3,456 m, guaranteeing snow from November to May. A paradise for technical skiing and freeride, the resort also offers a lively nightlife and high-end accommodation.

4Megève

rating 4.7/5
25334720

SnowFinder score of 4.7/5, for 220 km of runs between 1,113 and 2,350 m. Adult day lift pass: €65.

Megève is one of the most elegant resorts in the Alps, founded in 1916 by the Baroness de Rothschild to rival Saint-Moritz. This authentic Savoyard village at 1,113 m is as well known for its chic atmosphere and pedestrian centre as for its skiing (325 km with the Pays du Mont-Blanc). A wealthy clientele in an exceptional setting.

5Courchevel

rating 4.7/5
21413611

SnowFinder score of 4.7/5, for 150 km of runs between 1,100 and 2,738 m. Adult day lift pass: €77.

Courchevel is the most luxurious resort in France, spread over six villages from 1,300 to 1,850 m in the Trois Vallées (600 km). Its international clientele, palace hotels and starred restaurants make it a symbol of high-end skiing worldwide. The 150 km own ski area (109 runs) offers exceptional variety for all levels.

6Tignes

rating 4.7/5
6382016

SnowFinder score of 4.7/5, for 150 km of runs between 1,550 and 3,456 m. Adult day lift pass: €70.

Tignes was born in the 1950s after the filling of the Chevril dam. Together with Val d'Isère, it forms the Tignes-Val d'Isère ski area (300 km). Its Grande Motte glacier (3,456 m), open almost all year round, makes it a world reference. A car-free resort, hugely popular with snowboarders and freeriders, with one of Europe's best snowparks.

7La Plagne

rating 4.6/5
9783521

SnowFinder score of 4.6/5, for 225 km of runs between 1,250 and 3,250 m. Adult day lift pass: €72.

La Plagne is one of the most visited ski areas in the world, spread across 10 villages between 1,250 and 2,100 m. With Les Arcs, it forms Paradiski (425 km). It hosted the Olympic bobsleigh track at the 1992 Games, which is still open to the public. Very family-friendly, it offers a wide variety of runs and activities.

8Les Arcs

rating 4.6/5
10554216

SnowFinder score of 4.6/5, for 200 km of runs between 1,200 and 3,226 m. Adult day lift pass: €71.

Les Arcs is a purpose-built resort rising in tiers from 1,200 to 2,000 m, designed by Charlotte Perriand in the 1960s. With La Plagne it forms Paradiski (425 km), linked by the Vanoise Express. A pedestrian, family-friendly and accessible resort, it has 53 lifts and runs for all levels, set in a preserved Savoyard landscape.

9Méribel

rating 4.6/5
826288

SnowFinder score of 4.6/5, for 150 km of runs between 1,100 and 2,952 m. Adult day lift pass: €69.

Méribel lies at the heart of the Trois Vallées (600 km) at 1,100–2,952 m. Its uniform architecture in Savoyard wood and stone, required since its founding in 1938, gives it a rare character. It hosted the women's events at the 1992 Albertville Olympic Games. Its own 150 km ski area (70 runs) offers 85% of terrain above 1,800 m.

10Alpe d'Huez

rating 4.5/5
19171610

SnowFinder score of 4.5/5, for 250 km of runs between 1,135 and 3,330 m. Adult day lift pass: €68.

Alpe d'Huez is a legendary resort at 1,860 m in the Grandes Rousses massif, famous for its exceptional sunshine (300 days a year) and its Sarenne run (16 km, the longest black run in Europe). Its 250 km of runs climb to 3,330 m. The drive up from Bourg-d'Oisans is a legendary col on the Tour de France.
