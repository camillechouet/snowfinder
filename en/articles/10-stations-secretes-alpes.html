The 10 secret resorts in the Alps that are worth the detour

✍️ SnowFinder Editorial Team📅 March 2025⏱ 5 min read

Between giants like Courchevel or Val d'Isère, the Alps are home to lesser-known gems. These are resorts that have managed to stay authentic, accessible and, above all, less crowded.

1. Sainte-Foy-Tarentaise

The best-kept secret between Les Arcs and Val d'Isère. 25 km of runs for legendary off-piste skiing. Day lift pass for just €30.

2. Arêches-Beaufort

In the Beaufortain, natural snow is often exceptional. The stronghold of demanding skiers – and Beaufort AOP cheese as a bonus.

3. Bonneval-sur-Arc

The highest village in Savoie (1,800 m) beneath the col de l'Iséran. Listed architecture, timeless atmosphere.

4. Les Karellis

A unique community-run resort in France. Unbeatable prices, mountain spirit without the marketing.

5. La Grave - La Meije

No marked runs, 2,100 m of vertical drop. The world's Mecca of off-piste skiing. For experts with a guide.

6. Valfréjus

Facing the Vanoise, rarely crowded, with a varied and technical ski area that always surprises.

7. Aussois

A Vauban fortified village facing the Vanoise. 55 km of little-known runs in Haute-Maurienne.

8. Puy-Saint-Vincent

Gem of the Hautes-Alpes at the foot of the Écrins. Exceptional sunshine, with prices among the gentlest in the Southern Alps.

9. Saint-Véran

Highest municipality in Europe (2,042 m), a "Plus Beaux Villages de France" member. Skiing in the unspoilt Queyras.

10. Orelle

The little-known gateway to the 3 Vallées from the Maurienne. Direct access to Val Thorens and 600 km of the 3 Vallées.
