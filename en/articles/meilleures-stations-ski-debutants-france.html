The 10 best ski resorts for beginners in France

✍️ SnowFinder Editorial Team📅 9 November 2026⏱ 8 min

For a first week on skis, it's best to choose a ski area with gentle runs. We ranked the resorts in France by the share of green and blue runs in their ski area. We reviewed the 153 resorts in our database in France that qualify for this ranking.

Key takeaway: Les Arêtes is the easiest (80 % green and blue runs), ahead of Les Aillons-Margeriaz (79 % green and blue runs) · the 10th in the ranking, Praz-de-Lys Sommand, has 72 % green and blue runs.

The ranking: 10 resorts

1Les Arêtes

80 % green and blue runs
8840

8 green and 8 blue runs out of 20, i.e. 80 % easy runs, across a 20 km ski area. Adult day lift pass: 18 €.

Les Arêtes is a small family resort at 1,200 m in Savoie. Its 20 km ski area offers relaxed skiing in a preserved natural setting. A low-key resort with few visitors, ideal for a day on the slopes without the crowds.

2Les Aillons-Margeriaz

79 % green and blue runs
51432

5 green and 14 blue runs out of 24, i.e. 79 % easy runs, across a 22 km ski area. Adult day lift pass: 22 €.

Les Aillons-Margériaz is a family resort in the Bauges massif. The historic sector “Aillons-Margériaz 1000” (945 m) permanently closed its lifts in March 2025 after several winters of recurring snow shortage, and is turning to year-round activities. Alpine skiing is now concentrated entirely on the “Aillons-Margériaz 1400” sector, which is higher and has better snow. It offers 22 km of mostly blue runs, ideal for families and intermediate skiers, with views of Mont Blanc.

3Le Mont Dore

76 % green and blue runs
141162

14 green and 11 blue runs out of 33, i.e. 76 % easy runs, across a 41 km ski area. Adult day lift pass: 43 €.

Le Mont Dore is a spa and ski resort at 1,050 m, at the foot of the Puy de Sancy (1,886 m), the highest point of the Massif Central. Its 35 km of runs spread across the volcanic slopes. A spa town famous since the 19th century, it combines skiing and spa treatments in a remarkable neo-Gothic setting.

13 green and 6 blue runs out of 25, i.e. 76 % easy runs, across an 18 km ski area. Adult day lift pass: 16 €.

Font d'Urle is a plateau resort in the Vercors, at 1,430 m, geared towards Nordic and family skiing. Its 18 km of alpine runs are complemented by a Nordic area. The wild natural landscape of the Vercors gives it a timeless atmosphere.

5Les Saisies

75 % green and blue runs
1530123

15 green and 30 blue runs out of 60, i.e. 75 % easy runs, across an 83 km ski area. Adult day lift pass: 46 €.

Les Saisies is an unusual resort at 1,650 m on a panoramic plateau in the Beaufortain. Known for its 150 km of alpine skiing and its vast Nordic ski area, it hosted the cross-country skiing and biathlon events of the 1992 Albertville Olympic Games. The view of Mont Blanc from the runs is spectacular.

Hauteluce is a village in the Beaufortain at 1,150 m, with 50 km of runs linked to Les Saisies. Its baroque church and Savoyard chalets make it one of the most beautiful villages in Savoy. A family resort in a protected mountain setting, with views of Mont Blanc.

7Porte-Puymorens

74% of runs are green and blue
91772

9 green and 17 blue runs out of 35, i.e. 74% easy runs, across a 50 km ski area. Adult day lift pass: 40 €.

The lift pass is shared with the neighbouring resorts of <a href="formigueres.html" style="text-decoration:underline">Formiguères</a> and <a href="cambre-d-aze.html" style="text-decoration:underline">Cambre d'Aze</a> (Trio Pyrénées): same price, valid at your choice in either one. However, these are separate ski areas that are not connected by skis, so a pass only gives access to one resort per day. Porte-Puymorens is a resort at 1,600 m in the Pyrénées-Orientales, on the border with Andorra. Its 30 km of runs enjoy good snow cover thanks to the Col de Puymorens (1,920 m). A family-friendly resort close to Font-Romeu and Andorra for shopping.

Col de Rousset is a family resort at 1,254 m on the Vercors ridge, with an exceptional view over the Drôme and Provence. Its 20 km of runs are ideal for beginners. A remarkable natural setting within the Vercors Regional Natural Park.

Valmorel is a charming resort built in the 1970s in keeping with traditional Savoyard architecture, a model of its kind. At 1,400 m in the Tarentaise, its 165 km ski area extends as far as the Grand Domaine. A pedestrian, family-friendly resort, valued for its convivial atmosphere and carefully designed architecture.

What this ranking reveals

Les Arêtes tops the list with 80% green and blue runs, ahead of Les Aillons-Margeriaz. No. 10, Praz-de-Lys Sommand, still shows 72% green and blue runs.

A green run is very easy and a blue one is easy: the higher their share, the more forgiving the ski area is for your first turns. Also look at the beginner snow gardens, ski schools and beginner-friendly lifts before choosing.

Frequently asked questions

Which resort should I choose for a first ski holiday in France?

Les Arêtes tops our ranking with 80% green and blue runs, followed by Les Aillons-Margeriaz and Le Mont Dore.

What is a green or blue run?

A green run is very easy and a blue run is easy: they are the most suitable for your first days on skis.

Should I take lessons when I'm starting out?

Group lessons with an instructor help you progress faster and more safely; book them with your stay.

Conclusion

In short, Les Arêtes takes first place in this ranking in France (80% green and blue runs), and Praz-de-Lys Sommand closes the top 10 (72% green and blue runs). But run ease is only one criterion among others: atmosphere, ski area size, accommodation and your budget matter just as much when choosing.
