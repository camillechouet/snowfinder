Top 5 resorts for introducing your children to skiing

✍️ SnowFinder editorial team📅 February 2025⏱ 4 min read

Putting skis on children for the first time is a precious moment. Choosing the right resort makes all the difference between a budding passion and a lasting trauma.

1. Les Gets (Portes du Soleil)

Famille Plus label, an authentic village and access to the 650 km cross-border ski area once the children are progressing. The reference.

2. Valmorel

Car-free resort, Savoyard architecture, Famille Plus. Everything is designed with children in mind from the very design of the resort.

3. Villard-de-Lans (Vercors)

30 minutes from Grenoble, Famille Plus label, Nordic skiing, ice rink... A winter playground with very affordable prices.

4. Les Contamines-Montjoie

At the foot of Mont Blanc, in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Peaceful and natural, with a much-appreciated childcare service.

5. Les Saisies (Beaufortain)

Birthplace of Olympic cross-country skiing, an excellent family alpine ski area, Famille Plus. Authentic mountain life without the stress.
