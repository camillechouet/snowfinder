Les Carroz vs Samoëns: which resort to choose?

Full comparison: ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere, budget. Our verdict to help you decide.
Two neighbouring villages on the Grand Massif (265 km), linked by the same ski area but with distinctly different atmospheres. Les Carroz, perched on its sunny plateau, and Samoëns, a village designated a 'Pays d'Art et d'Histoire' at the bottom of the valley: here is what you need to help you choose your base camp.

👉 Both villages ski on the same ski area: see the full Grand Massif page.

👤 Which resort for which profile?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Families with young children
Les Carroz

Compact village resort right at the foot of the slopes, handy for avoiding repeated journeys with young children.

🏘️ Village charm and heritage
Samoëns

Designated a 'Pays d'Art et d'Histoire' and voted the most beautiful village in Haute-Savoie in 2021, with its medieval square and alpine botanic garden: a particularly well-kept setting for life off the slopes.

🎿 Direct access to the slopes from the village
Les Carroz

Located at 1140 m, directly on the ski area, compared with 710 m for Samoëns, which requires a gondola to reach the slopes.

🌤️ Skiing late in the season
Les Carroz

Higher base altitude, which slows melting at resort level a little longer than in Samoëns.

🍽️ Village life off the slopes
Samoëns

Restaurants, shops and events are more extensive year-round, supported by the charm of the designated village.

💶 Accommodation budget
Samoëns

Being a little further from the snow front, accommodation there is generally more affordable than in Les Carroz, right in the heart of the ski area.

💡 Our verdict

Both villages share the same ski area, so the choice comes down mainly to the atmosphere you're after. Les Carroz suits those who want to ski right from their accommodation. Samoëns is made for those who prefer a genuine village setting, even if it means taking a gondola to reach the slopes.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Are Les Carroz and Samoëns linked for skiing?

Yes, both villages are part of the Grand Massif (265 km of runs), skiable on a single lift pass, alongside Flaine, Morillon and Sixt-Fer-à-Cheval.

What's the best base for beginners?

Les Carroz is located directly on the ski area, which makes journeys easier with beginners or young children.

Is Samoëns a ski-in/ski-out resort?

Not directly: Samoëns is a valley village connected to the ski area by a gondola, unlike Les Carroz, which is ski-in/ski-out.

Is Samoëns one of the Plus Beaux Villages de France?

No, it isn't an official 'Plus Beaux Villages de France' (only Yvoire holds that label in Haute-Savoie). Samoëns does, however, have the 'Pays d'Art et d'Histoire' designation and was voted the most beautiful village in Haute-Savoie in 2021.
