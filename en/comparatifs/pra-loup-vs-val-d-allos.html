Pra-Loup vs Val d'Allos: which resort to choose?

Full comparison: ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere, budget. Our verdict to help you decide.
The two resorts that make up the Espace Lumière (180 km), in the Ubaye valley. Pra-Loup, a modern resort with carefully designed architecture, focuses on big, sunny skiing. Val d'Allos, more village-like with its two sectors Le Seignus and La Foux, keeps a preserved Provençal charm.

👤 Which resort for which profile?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Families with young children
Val d'Allos

Famille Plus label, the gentle and compact Le Seignus sector, and a reassuring village atmosphere for first steps on skis.

🎿 Intermediate/advanced skiers looking for big-mountain skiing
Pra-Loup

100 km of runs against 80 km at Val d'Allos, with a larger ski area and more red and black runs on the heights of the Grande Séolane.

☀️ Sunshine and light snow from the south
Pra-Loup

Exposure and altitude guarantee the dry snow typical of the southern Alps, with reliable snow cover up to 2,600 m.

🏘️ Village atmosphere and authenticity
Val d'Allos

Two villages, Le Seignus and La Foux, have kept a more human scale than the integrated resort of Pra-Loup, with a genuine Provençal setting.

🏂 Snowpark and freestyle
Pra-Loup

Equipped with a snowpark, the resort appeals more to freestyle enthusiasts than Val d'Allos, which is geared more towards classic skiing.

💰 Value for money
Val d'Allos

Slightly cheaper lift pass (35€ against 36€) and often more affordable accommodation, for a ski area within the same skiing basin.

💡 Our verdict

Since they share the same ski area, the Espace Lumière, the question is less which is better than where to unpack your bags. Pra-Loup suits independent skiers who want to explore a large, sunny ski area with a proper snowpark. Val d'Allos is the best base for a family looking for a genuine Provençal village and relaxed skiing.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Are Pra-Loup and Val d'Allos linked for skiing?

Yes, the two resorts form the Espace Lumière (180 km of runs), skiable without limit on a shared lift pass.

Which is the larger of the two resorts?

Pra-Loup, with 100 km of its own runs, against 80 km for Val d'Allos. Together they form a 180 km ski area.

Which one should I choose for a family holiday with young children?

Val d'Allos, which holds the Famille Plus label, with the Le Seignus sector particularly suited to beginners and the very young.

Is snow reliable in the Ubaye?

Yes, despite its southerly location, the altitude (up to 2,600 m across both resorts) and aspect generally guarantee dry snow and reliable snow cover from December to April.
