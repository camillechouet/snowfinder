Saint-Martin-de-Belleville vs La Tania: which resort should you choose?

Full comparison: ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere, budget. Our verdict to help you decide.
Two villages in the 3 Vallées, two gateways to the 600 km linked ski area. Saint-Martin-de-Belleville showcases its preserved Savoyard architecture and a lift pass priced at 67 €. La Tania, a car-free resort nestled in the forest between Courchevel and Méribel, focuses on calm, families and a summit at 2,732 m.

👤 Which resort for which profile?

👨‍👩‍👧 Families with young children
La Tania

The resort is car-free, has a childcare service and promotes a family atmosphere. With 21 green runs, it also offers more terrain for first turns.

💰 Skiers watching the price of their lift pass
Saint-Martin-de-Belleville

Its lift pass is priced at 67 € against 77 € at La Tania, a difference of 10 € (13 %).

⛷️ Confident skiers looking for steep slopes
La Tania

It has 11 black and 36 red runs, against 8 and 23 at Saint-Martin-de-Belleville. Its maximum altitude of 2,732 m is 332 m higher than its neighbour's.

🏡 Lovers of heritage and old stone villages
Saint-Martin-de-Belleville

A Savoyard village with preserved stone architecture, it presents itself as less touristy than its neighbours in the 3 Vallées.

✨ Upmarket stay
Saint-Martin-de-Belleville

Its listing associates it with a luxury atmosphere and highlights distinguished local gastronomy.

🗺️ Great explorers of the 3 Vallées
Tie

Both resorts give access to the 600 km of the 3 Vallées: Saint-Martin-de-Belleville via its own lifts linked to the Les Menuires sector, La Tania via two lifts up to the Col de la Loze.

💡 Our verdict

Saint-Martin-de-Belleville suits skiers looking for a real Savoyard village, a calmer atmosphere and a lift pass that is 10 € cheaper. Its advertised ski area reaches 160 km, against 150 km for its neighbour. La Tania will appeal mainly to families, thanks to its childcare and car-free setting, as well as to good skiers, who will find more red and black runs and a summit at 2,732 m. In both cases, the 600 km of the 3 Vallées remain accessible.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Which resort has the cheapest lift pass between Saint-Martin-de-Belleville and La Tania?

Saint-Martin-de-Belleville, with a lift pass priced at 67 € against 77 € at La Tania, so 10 € less (13 %).

Can you ski across the whole of the 3 Vallées from these two resorts?

Yes. Saint-Martin-de-Belleville is linked to the Les Menuires sector by its own lifts, and La Tania gives access to the Col de la Loze via two lifts. Both therefore open onto the 600 km of the 3 Vallées.

Which one is best suited to children?

La Tania, which is car-free, offers a childcare service and has 21 green runs, against 12 at Saint-Martin-de-Belleville.

Which resort goes highest?

La Tania peaks at 2,732 m, 332 m more than Saint-Martin-de-Belleville, whose maximum altitude is 2,400 m.
