Saint-Véran vs Molines-en-Queyras: which resort to choose?

Full comparison: ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere, budget. Our verdict to help you decide.
Saint-Véran and Molines-en-Queyras are not two linked ski areas: in fact they form one single ski area (35 km, single lift pass at 40 €), spread across the two villages. Saint-Véran, the highest commune in Europe (2042 m) and classed as a Plus Beau Village de France, occupies the upper part of the ski area with its steeper slopes. Molines-en-Queyras, at the bottom at 1740 m, offers gentler skiing through the forest, ideal for families.

👤 Which resort for which profile?

🏔️ Altitude and steeper slopes
Saint-Véran

Highest permanently inhabited commune in Europe (2042 m), located at the top of the ski area with more demanding skiing and an exceptional view of Mont Viso.

🏘️ Heritage and village charm
Saint-Véran

Officially classed as a Plus Beau Village de France, with its traditional fustes houses and carved sundials.

🌲 Forest skiing and gentle slopes for beginners
Molines-en-Queyras

Bottom of the ski area at 1740 m, skiing through the forest with a snow front designed for children — the style is reminiscent of the neighbouring village of Arvieux.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Families with young children
Molines-en-Queyras

Snow garden and a drag lift for beginners right at the bottom of the ski area, with no need to climb to altitude.

⛷️ Nordic skiing
Both villages on an equal footing

The Vallée des Aigues Nordic area (around 19 to 25 km depending on the trails) links the two villages and doesn't belong to either one in particular.

💰 Budget
No difference

Both villages ski on the same ski area with exactly the same lift pass (40 €): price is no longer a deciding factor here.

💡 Our verdict

Since it's the same ski area and the same lift pass, the question is not which one is "the best" but where to base yourself. Saint-Véran will suit those who want altitude, classed heritage and slightly more sporty skiing. Molines-en-Queyras is the logical choice for a family with young children, with gentler forest skiing at the bottom of the ski area.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Are Saint-Véran and Molines-en-Queyras linked for skiing?

Yes, and more than that: they actually form a single interconnected ski area (the Beauregard alpine area), spanning 35 km between 1740 m (Molines) and 2830 m, with one lift pass shared by both villages.

Do Saint-Véran and Molines have the same lift pass price?

Yes, exactly the same: a single adult day lift pass at 40 € gives access to the whole ski area, whichever village you start from.

Is Saint-Véran really classed as a Plus Beau Village de France?

Yes, Saint-Véran is one of the member villages of the official association Les Plus Beaux Villages de France.

What is the highest commune in Europe?

Saint-Véran, at 2042 m, is the highest permanently inhabited commune in Europe.

Where to ski as a family with young children?

The lower part of the ski area on the Molines-en-Queyras side (1,740 m) is skiable through forest, with a snow front designed for beginners, a bit like neighbouring Arvieux.
