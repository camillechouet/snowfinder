Métabief Mont d'Or vs Le Lioran: which resort to choose?

Full comparison: ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere, budget. Our verdict to help you decide.
Métabief Mont d'Or
Le Lioran
The clash of two mid-mountain giants: the Franco-Swiss Jura versus the volcanic Massif Central. Métabief, the largest resort in the Jura, banks on views of the Alps and a family-friendly, Nordic atmosphere. Le Lioran, the largest ski area in Auvergne, plays the card of volcanism and more technical skiing.

👤 Which resort for which profile?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Families with young children / beginners
Métabief Mont d'Or

Label Famille Plus, a gentle snow front, a high proportion of blue and green runs, and an immense Nordic area right next door to vary the fun.

🎿 Intermediate skiers looking for vertical drop
Le Lioran

60 km of runs against 26 km at Métabief, with a more pronounced vertical drop on the flanks of the Plomb du Cantal (1,855 m): more red and black runs to get your skis into.

🌋 Landscapes and geological uniqueness
Le Lioran

Skiing on the flanks of an ancient volcano dormant for 10,000 years, with views of the Monts du Cantal, offers a unique setting in France.

🇨🇭 Views and cross-border atmosphere
Métabief Mont d'Or

From the summit of the Mont d'Or (1,463 m), the view extends on clear days as far as Lac Léman and the Swiss Alps — a panorama that Le Lioran cannot offer.

💰 Small budget / short stay
Métabief Mont d'Or

Day pass slightly cheaper (25€ against 28€) and a more compact ski area, easy to cover in a day without feeling short-changed.

❄️ Most reliable snow
Le Lioran

Higher summit altitude (1,850 m against 1,423 m) and snow often colder and powdery thanks to the continental Auvergne climate.

💡 Our verdict

Two fine gateways to skiing outside the Alps, but for different wishes. Métabief Mont d'Or is the smart choice for a family wanting an easy ski area, a view of the Swiss Alps and a real Nordic playground on top. Le Lioran will appeal to skiers who want a larger, more technical ski area and a genuine volcanic Auvergne experience.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Métabief or Le Lioran: which is the bigger resort?

Le Lioran is bigger, with 60 km of runs against 26 km at Métabief Mont d'Or. Le Lioran remains, however, the largest resort in the Massif Central, while Métabief is the largest in the Jura massif.

Can you see the Alps from Métabief?

Yes, on clear days, the summit of the Mont d'Or (1,463 m) offers a clear view over Lac Léman and the Swiss Alps, one of the resort's great assets.

Which resort is best for beginners?

Métabief Mont d'Or, with a majority of green and blue runs and a compact ski area that is easy to get to grips with. Le Lioran also has a good beginners' area, but its larger ski area is better suited to intermediate levels.

Do Le Lioran and Métabief have a Nordic area?

Yes, both do. Métabief is part of a large Jura Nordic network, while Le Lioran makes the most of the wide open spaces of the Cantal for cross-country skiing and snowshoeing, in a preserved volcanic setting.
