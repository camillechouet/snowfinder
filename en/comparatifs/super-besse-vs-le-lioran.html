Super Besse vs Le Lioran: which resort to choose?

Full comparison: ski area, snow reliability, atmosphere, budget. Our verdict to help you decide.
The head-to-head of the Massif Central's two heavyweights. Super Besse in the heart of the Sancy, lively and family-friendly. Le Lioran facing the Plomb du Cantal, the largest ski area in Auvergne and a raw volcanic atmosphere.

👤 Which resort for which profile?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Families with beginner children
Super Besse

Wide snow front, several play areas, active ESF ski school, children's activities in the village (lake, sledging, snowshoeing). Logistically, it's simpler.

🎿 Skiers looking for a real ski area in the Massif Central
Le Lioran

60 km of runs, the largest in Auvergne. A more varied and more extensive ski area than Super Besse (43 km).

🌋 Authentic Auvergne / volcanic landscapes
Le Lioran

Spectacular views of the Plomb du Cantal and the Puy Mary, with a rawer, more unspoilt atmosphere. Super Besse feels more like a resort, Le Lioran more like the mountains.

🚗 Weekends from Clermont / Lyon / Paris
Super Besse

Clermont in 1h, Lyon in 3h, Paris in 5h. Le Lioran is 1h further from Clermont (Southern Cantal).

❄️ Most reliable snow
Le Lioran

Higher position (1,255-1,850 m vs 1,300-1,850 m for Besse) and a northern aspect. Le Lioran generally holds its snow a little better mid-season.

🎉 Evening entertainment / resort life
Super Besse

More bars, restaurants and evening events. Le Lioran is very quiet once the lifts close.

💡 Our verdict

Super Besse is the right choice for a family on a weekend or for those who want a lively, well-equipped resort. Le Lioran will appeal to independent skiers looking for a real ski area, volcanic scenery and a more authentic atmosphere. For a discovery stay in the Massif Central, the ideal is to combine the two: 4 days at Besse, 2 days at Le Lioran.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Super Besse or Le Lioran: which is the largest ski area?

Le Lioran, with 60 km of runs, is the largest ski area in the Massif Central. Super Besse offers around 43 km of runs – a respectable ski area, but a smaller one.

Which resort is more accessible from Paris or Lyon?

Super Besse is closer: about 5h from Paris and 3h from Lyon. Le Lioran adds around ~1h more as it lies further south in the Cantal.

Is snow reliable at Le Lioran and Super Besse?

Both resorts enjoy good snow cover from mid-December to mid-March in a normal season, with a slight edge for Le Lioran (slightly higher altitude and northern aspect). Both also have snow cannons to secure the lower part of the ski area.

Which resort to choose for children who are learning to ski?

Super Besse has a slight edge: a wider snow front, more visible fun parks, and a particularly active ESF school for children's lessons. But Le Lioran also offers very good beginner facilities on the Font d'Alagnon side.
