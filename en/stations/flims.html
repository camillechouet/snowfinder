Practical information

Flims is a ski resort in the Northern Alps range, in Surselva, Graubünden, Switzerland 🇨🇭. The ski area covers 176 km of runs between 1,080 m and 3,018 m, served by 29 lifts. Day lift pass 92€ per adult.

* Price per adult for 1 day, public high-season rate. Reduced rates (online purchase, outside school holidays, families, children, seniors) are generally offered by the resort.

ℹ️ These figures refer to the Flims Laax Falera ski area, which is shared by several resorts: kilometres of runs, number of runs, lifts and lift pass are common to all of them. The values shown here and on the neighbouring resort pages therefore describe the same ski area and the same lift pass, so they are not added together.

About Flims

The 176 km, 29 lifts and lift pass shown are those of the Flims Laax Falera ski area, shared by Laax, Flims and Falera. The day price is dynamic or not published: the price shown is an estimate. The run breakdown is given as a percentage of the kilometres for each difficulty level. Flims is a village in the Surselva linked to Laax and Falera within the Flims Laax Falera ski area. It lies near Lake Cauma, with its turquoise waters, and above the Rhine gorge, the Ruinaulta. The day price is dynamic: the price shown is an estimate.

✍️ Our verdict on Flims

The Flims ski area, with its 176 km of runs, offers a fine range for all kinds of skiers: big enough not to go round in circles for a week, yet compact enough to find your favourite sectors easily. The 29 lifts let you link runs without long queues. Flims' family reputation is well earned: dedicated beginner areas, a well-organised childcare service, well-groomed green runs and a friendly atmosphere at the foot of the lifts. It is one of the most reassuring resorts in the Northern Alps for laying the foundations of skiing with children. As for snow, Flims is in a strong position with its highest point at 3,018 m: even in capricious winters, the upper runs hold up well until April. The village at 1,080 m is also well placed to provide comfortable conditions from mid-December. A stay in Flims is clearly expensive: 92€ for a day lift pass, plus accommodation and mountain restaurants that are among the dearest in the Alps. It is the price of excellence, but you should be aware of it before you go. In short: Flims keeps its promises. A Northern Alps resort that will suit most skiers without disappointing.
